### Industry

**Amazon**, Barcelona (2019 to present), Senior Applied Scientist.

- Extracting and summarizing information from customer reviews, including the
  generative-AI review summaries launched on amazon.com.
- Applied research on summarization, multilinguality, and automatic evaluation of LLMs,
  published at venues such as ACL and EMNLP.
- Mentoring applied science interns.

### Research

From September 2015 to February 2019 I was a postdoc with [Ivan Titov](http://ivan-titov.org/), first at the
[Institute for Logic, Language and Computation](https://www.illc.uva.nl) of the University of Amsterdam
and then at the [School of Informatics](https://www.ed.ac.uk/informatics) of the University of Edinburgh.

From September 2009 to July 2015 I was a researcher in the Human Language Technology group at
[ISTI-CNR](https://www.isti.cnr.it) in Pisa, working with Fabrizio Sebastiani and [Andrea Esuli](http://www.esuli.it).
